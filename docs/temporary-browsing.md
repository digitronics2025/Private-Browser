# Temporary browsing

From a regular Account Space, open the browser menu and choose **New temporary
browsing window**. Enter an HTTP or HTTPS address. The window keeps its own
cookies, website storage and back/forward history in memory. Pop-ups and
downloads are blocked. Vault, AI, site permissions and other Account Space
tools are unavailable in this window. Links from other Windows apps continue
to open in the regular browser under its external-link setting.

**Keep bookmark** reviews the current title and the address without its query
or fragment. Confirm to add the bookmark to the Account Space that opened the
temporary window. Keep that Account Space selected to save. Protected pages
cannot be kept. Closing the temporary window discards its data. The main
browser also closes it when Banking becomes active or auto-lock takes effect.
