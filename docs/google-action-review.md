# Review Google actions

Open the connected Account Space and choose **Manage Account Spaces**. In
**Google overview**, choose **Reply** on a Gmail header or **Share** on an
allowed Drive file. **Schedule a meeting** prepares an event in the linked
account's primary calendar. These actions require the separate Gmail Send,
Drive Files or Calendar Write permissions as applicable.

The reply loads the original message headers for addressing and threading.
Enter your own reply. For a share, choose the recipient and Viewer or Editor.
For a meeting, add the times, guests and optional Google Meet link. **Review
action** shows the exact account, destination and contents. Edit the draft or
use the confirmation button to apply the displayed changes.

The confirmation expires after five minutes. It is tied to the exact payload
and can be used once. Before sending, the browser records an encrypted receipt
in the Account Space. It contains a fingerprint and outcome metadata. The
browser keeps up to 100 recent receipts and matches exact retries within 24
hours, including after restart. A confirmed receipt can show the Google
reference. For an unconfirmed result, check the original Google app before
acting there. Browser close, lock and account switching remove the draft from
view. Recipient addresses, reply text and meeting details are absent from the
receipt. Banking does not offer these actions.
